Linux-Help provides a means of real-time collaboration and communication through the use of [Discord](https://discord.gg/ANmuR8zWTt) and Libera.Chat networks IRC service, the channel [#Linux-Help](irc://libera.chat/#linuxhelp).

## Rules for ##Linux-Help are:

* **Respect each other.**  
    This group is a helpful bunch and there is no need to get hostile towards anyone. This channel is intended for real time collaboration and help, and though nobody is directly paid for this help, it's just nice to keep things 
civil and respectful.
* **Keep it clean.**  
    Please keep the level of vulgarity down to minimums, this helps to keep a respectful nature of this channel.
* **English is primary.**  
    Though this channel is primarily English oriented, it's acceptable to ask if others are more familiar with your native language, but we ask you to conduct your topic outside the channel as soon as possible, this allows the channel to continue as normal without interuption of it's normal communications.
* **No flooding, shouting (all-caps usage), or intentional annoyance or hostilities.**  
    All of these takes away from the value of the channel and distracts or otherwise takes away from the respectful and helpful nature of the channel.
