Linux-Help Cooperative is a group of helpful Linux users and professionals that are here to help and teach people the uses of linux for various aspects of it.

This organization is founded by Eric Renfro (Psi-Jack on IRC) as a means for like-minded people to work together in helping the Linux community as a whole and provide a means to teach people the principals and values of Linux.

This website is intended for all audiences wanting to learn how to do practically anything with Linux, from basic day-to-day desktop use, to the more advanced administrative and engineering techniques used by top professionals in the Linux professional world.

Check out what we have to offer in the following sections of our site:

* [Wiki](/wiki): Hands-on knowledge base that is intended to be easy to understand and follow.
* [Forums](https://forum.linux-help.org/): Community communication platform to provide Q&amp;A assistance for people.
* [Chat](/chat): Real-time communication platform for members and guests to use to discuss Linux and Linux-Help's website.
* [Donate](/donate): Provide contributions of various kinds for continued support and hosting for this site.
* [About](/about): About Linux-Help.org staff and goals.
