Linux-Help is a community of helpful Linux users and professionals that are here to help and teach people the uses of linux for various aspects of it.

This website is intended for all audiences wanting to learn how to do practically anything with Linux, from basic day-to-day desktop use, to more advanced administrative and engineering techniques used by top professionals in the Linux professional world. Our Wiki provides hands-on knowledge that are intended to be easy to understand and follow, hopefully even more than the rest of the internet combined. Our forums provides a means to communicate and ask questions, along with our IRC channel we run on Freenode network, <a class="urlextern" href="irc://chat.freenode.net/##linux-help" rel="nofollow" title="irc://chat.freenode.net/##linux-help">##linux-help</a>.
